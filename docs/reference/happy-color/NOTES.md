# Happy Color reference (screenshots by Kandy, 2026-10-23)

Observed in the desktop app. Reference for feel only; their artwork is theirs.

- **My Studio**: pink UI, "Save progress" button, trophy; three tabs (in progress / completed / other); completed pages shown as square thumbnails with a green check badge. Bottom nav: My Studio, Daily, Library, Search, More.
- **Page**: square, pure black ink line art on white. Numbers are small and only drawn where a region is big enough at the current zoom; many small regions are unnumbered until you zoom in. Palettes are large: numbers seen up to 142 on one page.
- **Selecting a colour** highlights every region of that number on the page: one page used a light tint of the colour (salmon for colour 4), another a grey crosshatch pattern. This is how you find what's left.
- **Palette bar**: a horizontally scrolling row of circles with the colour and its number; the selected circle is raised/larger. Numbers in the circle use dark or light text for contrast.
- **Hints**: lightbulb with a counter (3) top right; back button top left.
- **Reveal**: filling a region shows the artist's shaded colour inside the outline (gradients included), not a flat swatch; outlines stay on top.
